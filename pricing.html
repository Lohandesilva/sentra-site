Payment

How payment works, step by step

No surprises. You see the scope and the price in writing before any money moves, and every payment is a PayPal invoice you can check from inside your own PayPal account.

  1. Free 20-minute walkthrough

    We go through how variations get from site to claim in your business today and whether this fits.

    Cost: nothing

  2. Written scope and fixed quote

    You get a document with what's in, what's out, the monthly price, the go-live date and what you're agreeing to. Take your time with it.

    Cost: nothing

  3. Accept the scope: first month

    When you accept the scope, we send a PayPal invoice for the first month. There is no setup fee: workflow mapping, call scripts and connecting your job system are included.

    Pay: first month

  4. Go-live on the date in your scope

    Calls start running on your live jobs and variation notes land in your job system as scoped. The go-live date is written into the scope.

    Cost: nothing extra

  5. Monthly after that

    After the first month, the monthly rate is invoiced in advance by PayPal. Cancel with 30 days' written notice.

    Pay: monthly rate

Secure payments with PayPal

  • Every payment is an itemised PayPal invoice that quotes the same scope number as your written quote.
  • Pay with your PayPal balance, linked bank account or card. Your card and bank details go to PayPal. Sentra never sees or stores them.
  • You can open the invoice in your own PayPal account and check it before you pay.
  • PayPal's dispute process applies to PayPal payments, subject to PayPal's own terms. See PayPal's buyer protection terms for what is and isn't covered.
  • We never ask for payment by direct deposit to a personal account, gift cards or crypto. If an invoice doesn't come from PayPal quoting your scope number, don't pay it. Ring us first on the numbers on the contact page.
Questions

Common questions

What if a foreman doesn't answer the call?

The call is retried within the agreed call window. If there's still no answer, the office summary lists that job as "no response" so someone can ring the foreman directly.

Which job systems and ERPs does it work with?

Anything with an API or a reliable import route, or a structured spreadsheet or CSV handoff. We confirm the exact route in the walkthrough and write it into the scope before you pay anything.

Does it price or lodge variations for us?

No. It records what the foreman reports and flags what's missing. Your PM or estimator prices it, gets the paperwork in order and lodges it. Nothing is approved or claimed automatically.

What happens if we have more than 40 foremen, or need something different?

That's Custom Development. Book a free consultation and we'll scope it and quote a fixed price.

Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Packages are month-to-month with 30 days' written notice, and there's no setup fee. Fees already paid for a month in progress are not refunded, except as required by law.

Where does our data go, and who can see it?

Your job data and call transcripts are used only to deliver your service. The Legal and Security page covers how calls, data and payments are handled.

Not sure which package fits?

Tell us how many foremen you have and what system you run jobs in. We'll point you to the right package or scope a custom build, free.
