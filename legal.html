Security

We handle job records, call transcripts and contact details that sit close to your commercial position. These are the practices we follow to protect them.

  • Encryption in transit. Connections to our site and to the systems that run your service use TLS.
  • Encryption at rest. Data we store on your behalf is encrypted at rest by our hosting providers.
  • Least-privilege access. Connections to your ERP or job system use scoped credentials limited to the fields agreed in your written scope.
  • Draft, not destructive. Write-back to your job system adds draft variation notes. It does not approve, price or lodge anything, and it does not delete or overwrite your existing records.
  • Protected accounts. Administrative access to our systems and hosting accounts is protected with multi-factor authentication.
  • Segregated by client. Your jobs, transcripts and summaries are kept separate from other clients' data.
  • Incidents. If we find an incident that affects your data, we'll tell you promptly and explain what happened and what we're doing about it.

Sentra does not claim third-party security certifications. If your IT team or head contractor sends a security questionnaire, we complete it in writing. Send it to lohan@sentratechnologies.com.

How your data is handled

What we handle

  • Job details read from your ERP or job system: job numbers, sites, assigned foremen, contract details and approved variations.
  • Foreman check-in calls: the audio, a time-stamped transcript and the structured notes taken from them.
  • Office summaries sent to your PMs, estimators and office staff.
  • Contact and billing details for your business.

How we use it

Only to deliver and support your service. We don't sell your data and we don't use it to build marketing lists.

Service providers

To run the service we use third-party providers for telephony, speech processing, language processing, hosting and payments. They process data on our behalf to deliver your service. A current list is available on request.

Overseas handling

Sentra operates from Sri Lanka, and our providers may process data in other countries. Where we disclose personal information overseas to deliver the service, we take reasonable steps to make sure it's handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Your hosting arrangements are confirmed in your written scope.

Retention and deletion

Call audio, transcripts and job data are kept for the period set out in your scope. On request, and when your service ends, we delete your data and confirm in writing once it's done, except where we must keep records by law.

Calls and recording

  • Calls identify themselves. Each check-in call opens by saying who is calling, that it's an automated call on behalf of the foreman's employer, and that the call is recorded.
  • Recording is for a stated purpose. Recordings and transcripts are kept to document variations and for quality checks. We don't publish them or share them outside your business and the service providers that help run the service.
  • A person is always an option. A foreman who would rather not use the call can say so and the office gets a "no response" flag to follow up directly.
  • Your responsibility as the employer. You confirm your foremen know the check-in call is part of the job. We'll supply a short note you can give your crews.

Australian state and federal laws govern recording phone calls, and they vary by state. We design the calls to open with notice for that reason. This is a summary of how the service works, not legal advice.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal. Every payment is an itemised PayPal invoice quoting the scope number from your written quote. Your card and bank details go to PayPal, and Sentra never sees or stores them. The payment steps and monthly prices are laid out on the pricing page.

PayPal's dispute process applies to payments made through PayPal, subject to PayPal's own terms and eligibility rules. You can read them in PayPal's buyer protection terms. Sentra does not promise cover beyond what PayPal provides.

Staying safe from payment scams. We will never ask you to pay by direct deposit to a personal account, gift card or crypto. If an invoice doesn't come from PayPal and quote your scope number, don't pay it. Ring us on the numbers below first.

Privacy policy

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles where they apply to us.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, company, email, phone, trade and the size of your business, through the enquiry form, email, phone or the booking calendar.
  • Information processed in delivering your service, as described above, which can include names and work details of your foremen and office staff.
  • Basic site usage data, such as pages visited, to keep the site working.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiries, scope and deliver your service, invoice you, and support you afterwards.

Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email lohan@sentratechnologies.com and we'll respond. If you're not happy with how we've handled a request, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Terms of service

These terms summarise how we work together. They're confirmed in the written scope and quote you accept before the service starts. If there's a conflict, the signed scope wins.

The service

We provide a variation and daily project capture workflow as described in your written scope: scheduled check-in calls, structured notes written to your job system as drafts, and summaries to the people you nominate.

What the service does not do

The service records and flags. It does not price, approve or lodge variations, give legal or commercial advice, or decide what you're entitled to claim under your contracts. Those decisions stay with you.

Fees and payment

Fees are in Australian dollars and are charged monthly. There is no setup fee: onboarding is included in the monthly price. The first month is invoiced when you accept the written scope, and later months are invoiced monthly in advance. Payments are made by PayPal invoice. Any applicable taxes are shown on the invoice.

Term and cancelling

The service is month-to-month. Either side can end it with 30 days' written notice. Fees already paid for a month in progress are not refunded, except as required by law.

Your responsibilities

You give us access to the systems agreed in the scope, tell us promptly about changes to jobs and foremen, make sure your foremen know about the check-in calls, and review the draft variation notes before relying on them.

Your data and our work

Your job data and call content remain yours. Sentra keeps ownership of the software, scripts and methods used to deliver the service, and grants you the right to use the service for your business while you're a client.

Liability

Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law that can't be excluded. Subject to that, and to the extent the law allows, Sentra isn't liable for indirect or consequential loss, and our total liability for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

Governing law
