About Sentra

Enterprise software for commercial subcontractors

Most of a sub's margin is lost in small things that never make it to paper: a verbal instruction from the builder's super, an extra half-day on the tools, a relocation after the ceiling changed. The first workflow we've built targets exactly that: variation and daily project capture, from the foreman's phone to your job record and the PM's inbox.

Lohan De Silva leads discovery, build and support personally. You deal with the same person from the first walkthrough through to go-live and the monthly reviews.

How we work

  • Written scope before any payment. You see what's in, what's out and what it costs before a dollar moves.
  • Works with what you already run. We connect to your ERP or job system. We don't ask you to replace it.
  • Draft, never auto-approve. Sentra records and flags. Your PM and estimator price and lodge.
  • Plain communication. Walkthrough calls in Melbourne business hours, and straight answers on what it can and can't do.
